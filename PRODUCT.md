# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Employees and administrators of Kumwell. Employees use the system to request services, book assets (e.g. rooms, cars), follow up on request statuses, and read company news. Administrators use it to manage workflows, review applications, configure permissions, and post news/announcements.

## Product Purpose
To serve as the central portal for Human Assets Management & Service Building (HAMS) inside Kumwell, improving efficiency in handling employee requests (e.g. housing, booking) and corporate communications.

## Brand Personality
Professional, Trusted, and Efficient. Focus on corporate trust, highly usable layouts, clean form styling, clear hierarchies, and an identity aligned with the Kumwell corporate branding (primarily deep reds and supportive neutrals).

## Anti-references
Avoid overly complex animations, heavy gradient text, excessive glassmorphism, cramped typography, and distracting decorative elements that impede workflow efficiency.

## Design Principles
- **Clarity Over Clutter:** Interfaces must prioritize readability and clear labels. Information dense but easily readable.
- **Workflow Efficiency:** Reduce the steps required to complete common tasks (like requesting housing or booking a car).
- **Consistent Visual Hierarchy:** Consistent headings, color coding for statuses, and aligned buttons across all modules.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for standard body text.
- Keyboard navigability for forms and major dropdown menus.
- Clear error state cues that do not rely solely on color.
